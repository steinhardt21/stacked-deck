Stacked Deck

A deck laid over the page. Press Deal: the cards come up one at a time and the front card lands last. The deck opens at its middle, so the first scroll can go up or down. Turn it with the wheel, a swipe or the arrow keys, and press the front card to choose it.

The deal, timed to the fan

The loudness of card-fan-1 (the Dealer set's open sound), measured every 10 ms. The three cards behind land during the riffle, furthest first; the front card lands last, between the peak and the final tap.

  • 0–140 ms thumb on the deck
  • 150–450 ms riffle · cards 3, 2, 1 land (~330 / 400 / 470 ms)
  • 460–580 ms peak and tap · the front card lands (~520–570 ms)
