Stacked Deck

Press Deal. The cards come up one at a time, the front one last. Scroll, swipe or use the arrow keys to turn them, either way from the start. Press the front card to choose it.

Vanilla TypeScript, motion in CSS, sound from Web Audio — no runtime dependencies. Source on GitHub.

The stacked-in-depth interaction is inspired by Rauno Freiberg’s “Time Machine” on Devouring Details. This is an independent implementation.
